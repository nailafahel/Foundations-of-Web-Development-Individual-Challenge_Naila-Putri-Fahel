About Me

I started at the drawing board, not the keyboard, spending years learning to make ideas look good and feel intuitive before I set out to make them work through code. That path is what sets me apart: as an Informatics Engineering student at Universitas Pancasila with a certified multimedia background in graphic design, animation, and video editing, I combine the structured logic of an engineer with the instinctive eye of a designer.

Serving as a Teaching Assistant across several practicum courses deepened my technical foundation and taught me to break down complex ideas into something clear and approachable, a skill I value as much as writing the code itself. Today, my command of programming languages and design tools fuels my growing focus on web development, front-end engineering, and UI/UX design, the space where logic meets design and a creative background becomes a real advantage.
